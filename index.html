About

Jasmine was born and raised on the island of O'ahu, Hawai'i. She is currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in General Creative Media at the University of Hawai'i-West O'ahu, along with a Certificate in Music. With a passion for the arts, Jasmine has always enjoyed expressing her creativity through both performance and artistic creation. Outside of her academic pursuits, she is an independent music artist who writes, creates, and releases her music across various streaming platforms.
